01

Biography

Sheikh Abdulaziz Al-Qaeed is the Imam of Qasr Al-Hukm Mosque in the Riyadh Region. He served as Executive Director of the Riyadh Region Reconciliation Committee (1437–1444 AH), by decision of His Royal Highness the Emir of the Region, and formerly chaired its Family Protection Committee. Earlier, he led the Saqia Al-Maa Association in Riyadh and served as Imam of Al-Obaikan Mosque in Shubra. His work focuses on family reconciliation, community development, Quran memorization, and cultural, educational and awareness publications.
