Biography

Sheikh Abdulaziz Al-Qaeed has served as an imam since 1413 AH and previously led the Riyadh Region Reconciliation Committee as Executive Director. His work spans education, community development, media and writing.

Sheikh Abdulaziz Al-Qaeed is the Imam of Qasr Al-Hukm Mosque in the Riyadh Region. He served as Executive Director of the Riyadh Region Reconciliation Committee (1437–1444 AH), by decision of His Royal Highness the Emir of the Region, and formerly chaired its Family Protection Committee. Earlier, he led the Saqia Al-Maa Association in Riyadh and served as Imam of Al-Obaikan Mosque in Shubra. His work focuses on family reconciliation, community development, Quran memorization, and cultural, educational and awareness publications.

Reconciliation and family — Reconciliation, family protection and dispute settlement committees.

Community development — Community development associations, the Saqia Al-Maa Association and charitable foundations.

Quran and education — Quran memorization schools and houses, and educational training courses.

Media and culture — Cultural programs and forums, and media appearances on television channels.
