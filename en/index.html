Abdulaziz bin Abdulrahman bin Ibrahim Al-Qaeed

01

About

Imam of Qasr Al-Hukm Mosque in the Riyadh Region. He served as Executive Director of the Riyadh Region Reconciliation Committee (1437–1444 AH), by decision of His Royal Highness the Emir of the Region, and formerly as Chairman of the Region's Family Protection Committee. Earlier, he was Executive Director of the Saqia Al-Maa Association in Riyadh and Imam of Al-Obaikan Mosque in Shubra. His work focuses on family reconciliation, community development, Quran memorization, and cultural, educational and awareness publications.
