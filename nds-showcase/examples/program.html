Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence

Preparing future leaders in AI, machine learning, and data science through rigorous academics and hands-on research

About the Program

The Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence program equips students with a strong foundation in computer science, mathematics, and the core disciplines of AI including machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, and computer vision. The program emphasizes both theoretical understanding and practical application, preparing graduates for careers in industry, research, and entrepreneurship.

Students engage in hands-on projects, internships, and a capstone research experience. The curriculum is designed in alignment with international standards and industry needs, ensuring graduates are prepared to contribute to Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 digital transformation goals and the global AI ecosystem.

  • Build and train deep neural networks for tasks in vision, language, and reinforcement learning.
  • Evaluate AI models using appropriate metrics, statistical tests, and ablation studies.
